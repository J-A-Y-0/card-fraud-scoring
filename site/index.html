        What the score is

        A boosted-tree model over 60 point-in-time variables: how much each card, merchant and pairing has spent, how often and how suddenly, over the last 0–30 days.

        Why the block matters

        Fraud comes in runs on a compromised card. A confirmed alert blocks the card and every later fraud on it is declined. That, not the flagged purchase, is where the value is.

        Why the numbers hold

        The model and cutoffs were chosen on Jan–Oct. Everything here replays Nov–Dec, which no choice touched. With 16 cards the range is wide; the report quantifies it.
